# Getting your data

Interface labels change. Paths below are practical starting points and do not promise that every account sees the same menu. Export the widest date range that still matches the question, and keep the date range in the filename.

## TikTok Ads Manager

For account, campaign, ad group, ad, creative, audience, and placement performance, start in **Reporting**, **Analytics**, or the campaign table, depending on the current interface. Set the date range, choose the reporting level, add spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPM, CPC, reported conversions, CPA, conversion value, frequency, and video-view columns when they are available, then export CSV. For daily fatigue or CPA work, include date and ad ID. For search work, look for the Search Ads Campaign reporting view or search terms and keyword reporting available to the account. Save screenshots when a column cannot be exported.

For planned-edit and diagnosis work, look for account change history or the campaign activity history. If it is unavailable, paste a dated change log from the operator. Do not infer a change event from a chart alone.

## Events Manager

For Pixel and Events API checks, start in **Assets** or **Events Manager**, select the relevant data source, and capture the overview, event list, diagnostics, matching or connection status, test-event view, and event setup screen where available. Screenshots are enough. Include the site URL, date, and the event names the business treats as primary. Diagnostics are evidence of an implementation signal, not proof of lost revenue.

## TikTok Shop Seller Center

For GMV Max Profit Check, export order or settlement reports from **Finance**, **Orders**, **Data Compass**, **Affiliate**, **Promotions**, or comparable Seller Center reports available in the market. Bring product revenue, refunds, affiliate commission, coupon cost, referral fees, shipping charges borne by the seller, and ad spend for the same dates. Seller Center report names and fee categories vary by market, so label any missing line `needs_data` rather than treating it as zero.

## Independent reporting

For attribution comparison, export the same dates from GA4, Shopify, CRM, app analytics, or the order system. Record the conversion definition, timezone, attribution window, and whether the source is deduplicated. A TikTok Ads Manager conversion is not automatically comparable to an order, lead, or CRM-qualified sale.

## Creatives, pages, and comments

Download or screen-record the first seconds of the ad, then paste the spoken opening or on-screen text. For comments, export or paste comments with identifiers removed. For Landing Page Match, provide a mobile screenshot or page text plus the exact ad promise. Never paste customer personal data.

## A useful habit

Do not use an export whose date range is unclear. If the same dates, timezone, conversion definition, and attribution settings cannot be established, the output should say `needs_data` rather than present a comparison as fact.
