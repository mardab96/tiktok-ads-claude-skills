# AGENTS.md: TikTok Ads operator skills

This pack contains twenty data-first TikTok Ads skills. Every skill works from supplied exports, screenshots, scripts, comments, or independent reports. It stops at an approval-ready recommendation.

## Composition

Start an unfamiliar account with Account Triage. Verify measurement with Pixel and Events API Check plus Attribution Gap Check before CPA, waste, bid, or scale decisions. Use Creative Fatigue Detector, Hook Rate Scorer, Winning Ad Remix, and UGC Brief Writer as a sequence. Keep Learning Phase Guard between diagnosis and any live proposal. Use Weekly Client Report as a communication layer, then route uncertain movements to the owning specialist.

`tiktok-ads-shared/` belongs to this pack and prevents collision with other packs. It owns `references/`, `scripts/`, and `examples/`. Numeric rules live once in `tiktok-ads-shared/references/thresholds.md`; evidence and verdict vocabulary live in `tiktok-ads-shared/references/output-standard.md`.

## Vertical judgment

Ecommerce and TikTok Shop require refund, fee, COGS, shipping, stock, and margin evidence before a scale decision. Lead generation needs CRM-qualified outcomes and a longer lag. App work needs a mature in-app event plus app analytics and release history. A TikTok-reported conversion remains different from a profit result in every vertical.

## Live-action boundary

Skills never publish ads, change budgets or bids, alter tracking, turn placements on or off, approve Spark permissions, edit pages, or change Shop promotions. Preserve `needs_data`, conversion lag, and attribution mismatch in every output.
